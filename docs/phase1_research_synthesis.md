## G · Security & governance (municipal)
National backdrop (mid-2026): homicides rising (~+3–4%), armed groups grew ~20% to ~27,000 (Clan del Golfo largest), **Paz Total collapsed** (ELN table ended Apr 2026), hardline **de la Espriella won the Jun-2026 runoff** (inaugurated 7 Aug 2026). All three study municipalities are in the **safer half of Colombia, free of armed-group control.**
- **Tunja — score 86.** 4 homicides in 2025 (lowest of any Colombian capital; ~5.0/100k in 2022); no armed groups; institutional city.
- **Villa de Leyva — score 80 (town) / ~80 flank.** Boyacá decade-low homicides (44 dept-wide 2025), extortion down sharply; BUT persistent tourist-town property crime, a **Feb-2025 targeted robbery-murder of a US expat**, and **mayor jailed May-2026 for construction-licensing bribery** (permit-process risk).
- **El Colegio/Mesitas — score 70.** Cundinamarca = safest department (9.12/100k 2024), but peri-Bogotá organized crime: **Aug-2025 Mesitas massacre** (3 killed, finca-theft/"ajuste de cuentas"), Tren de Aragua microtráfico. Finca theft is a direct rural-estate risk.
Sources: FIP/Ideaspaz (homicidios, armed-group growth) ideaspaz.org; El Tiempo (Cundinamarca 60% zero-homicide muni; Mesitas massacre; expat murder) eltiempo.com; Boyacá 7 Días (decade-low homicides) boyaca7dias.com.co; Policía Nacional (Boyacá extortion −88%; Tunja lowest capital rate) policia.gov.co; Infobae (mayor jailing; de la Espriella win) infobae.com; Defensoría (Arauca–Cubará alert, ~300 km away, irrelevant) defensoria.gov.co.

## J · Legal, tenure & co-ownership
**Foreigners/non-residents may own rural land freely** (carve-outs: baldíos, resguardos, border zones — n/a here). Due-diligence spine: CTL via SNR/VUR (watch *falsa tradición*); **ANT baldío + UAF** (one-UAF accumulation cap can void a group buy if the parcel traces to a baldío — Ley 160/1994; nullity per CSJ SC877-2022); POT zoning vs Ley 2ª forest reserve / páramo (Ley 1930/2018) / **SFF Iguaque buffer** / **ronda hídrica** (≥30 m, Decreto 2245/2017); Villa de Leyva **PEMP/BIC** heritage reach (Res. Mincultura 2407/2020 — verify rural extent); **water concession** (Corpoboyacá / CAR Cundinamarca, Decreto 1076/2015). Acquisition costs ~2–4%.
**Co-ownership — recommend a Colombian SAS holding the land:** member exits transfer *shares* (no re-notarisation, no impuesto de registro on the land, no partition-forced-sale risk); shares **excluded from the non-resident wealth-tax base**; governance fully contractual. Costs: corporate renta 35% + ~20% dividend WHT on cash extraction, GMF 0.4%; a shareholder's death still triggers a Colombian succession of the **shares** (soften via lifetime nuda-propiedad or a foreign holdco). **Avoid plain copropiedad** long-term (per-death land succession + partition risk). Fiducia mercantil only if asset-protection justifies fiduciary fees.
Sources: Supernotariado certificados.supernotariado.gov.co; ANT apps.ant.gov.co; DNP baldíos cartilla; INCORA Res. 041/1996 (UAF); MinAmbiente Decreto 1076/2015, ronda hídrica; Mincultura PEMP Villa de Leyva; DIAN (RUT extranjeros, GMF); Ley 2277/2022 (ganancia ocasional 15%, dividend WHT); gerencie.com, estudiolegalhernandez.com, pgplegal.com.
