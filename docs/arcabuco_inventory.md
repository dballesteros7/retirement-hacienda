## Constraints that gate any Arcabuco parcel (from EOT / Parques / MADS)
- **OFF-LIMITS:** SFF Iguaque national park (6,889 ha) + delimited **Páramo Iguaque-Merchán** (MADS Res. 1555/2016; Ley 1930/2018 bans new build/ag). Covers Arcabuco's high **south/SW** (upper Quirvaquirá, Rupavita, Quemados, Monte Suárez heads, upper Centro) + **Serranía El Peligro** RFP on the E/SE. Wet forested spring-fed parcels are often exactly the restricted ones — overlay every candidate on SFF + páramo + El Peligro before offering.
- **Freely buildable belt:** central/near-town (lower Centro/Cabeceras), Peñas Blancas, lower Quirvaquirá, lower N slopes, and the **Arcabuco–Villa de Leyva corridor** lower elevations.
- **UAF ≈ 9.9 ha** → cannot subdivide rural land below it: buy ONE titled parcel held in a shared structure (SAS), don't carve lots.
- **Ronda hídrica** ~30 m no-build setback along every quebrada/nacimiento (spring-dense here → reduces net buildable area).
- **Water:** abundant rain ≠ legal potable source. Rural acueductos veredales often IRCA medium/high risk; a Corpoboyacá concession is still required. Verify per parcel.
- **Access/services:** Ruta 62 (Tunja–Arcabuco–Moniquirá, paved, toll at Arcabuco); Arcabuco↔Tunja ~45 min, ↔VdL ~35–45 min (paved 2025), ↔Bogotá ~3–3.5 h. Health = first-level centro de salud only (Tunja for anything higher); EBSA power; internet patchy rurally (Starlink fallback).
- **Diligence:** dated EOT (~2000, likely under revision — confirm current zoning at Planeación); title via SNR/VUR + ANT baldío check; informal tenure common.
