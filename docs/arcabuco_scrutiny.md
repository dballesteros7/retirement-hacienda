# Arcabuco wet flank — property scrutiny summary (all 9 candidates)

Photo galleries + terrain reviewed in-browser 19 Jul 2026. Full illustrated version = the `hacienda-arcabuco-dossier` artifact / `arcabuco_dossier.html`.

## The fork (pick climate first, then parcel)
- **Cool green highland (~2,100–2,700 m, ~13–16 °C)** — Arcabuco & Gachantivá: lush, cool, beautiful "wet flank," but **steep** → net-buildable flat area + protected-boundary proximity are the constraints.
- **Warm Moniquirá flank (~1,700 m, ~19 °C)** — north down paved Ruta 62: the **gentlest, cheapest, most genuinely buildable land** seen (open pasture, big shade trees) + abundant water, but a warm subtropical lifestyle and ~40 min farther from Villa de Leyva/Tunja.
- Gating constraints unchanged: SFF Iguaque park + delimited páramo (no-build; wettest parcels flirt with the edge) and UAF ≈ 9.9 ha (buy ONE parcel in a shared SAS).

## Cross-cutting insight
On the highland, **slope / net-buildable hectares is the decisive unknown** — nearly every parcel is wet enough; few are obviously flat. Moniquirá trades the cool climate for genuinely gentle, buildable land. Every parcel still needs: geotech (build pad), hydrogeology + Corpoboyacá water concession, protected-area overlay (SFF/páramo/El Peligro/ronda), title (SNR/ANT/UAF), and access/utilities checks. Listings mostly via ATOS Inmobiliaria (H via Casa 360, I via Rubén Quiroga).
